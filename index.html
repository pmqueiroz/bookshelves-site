A cozy board game for you and your friends.

Draft books from library carts, line them up by genre and fill your shelf for points. Up to 4 players online, with bots for the empty seats, plus a fresh Daily Curate puzzle every day.

Play with friends, or with anyone

Host a table and invite friends straight from Steam, or pick FIND A MATCH and sit at a public one. Bots take the empty seats, and anyone who joins takes a bot's place. No ports to open.

Daily Curate is a solo puzzle: the same set shelf and the same books for everyone, every day. Keep your streak going, climb the world leaderboard and watch the best runs as replays.

Cozy to pick up

On your turn, take every book of one genre from a cart; the rest slide to the rug. Line them up on your dresser and fill a row to send a book up to your shelf. A practice game against bots teaches the rules.
