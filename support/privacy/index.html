1. Who we are

Bookshelves is made by one person, Pedro Martins de Queiroz (peam). This page explains what the game and this website keep about you, where it goes, and what you can do about it. Questions and requests go to help@bookshelves.live.

2. What the game keeps

The game has no account or server of its own. It doesn't ask for your name, email or age, and it sends nothing to us: no analytics, no tracking, no crash reports.

To play it uses your Steam account: your Steam ID, display name and avatar, read from Steam.

3. Where it lives

In your Steam Cloud, private to your account: your coins, what you bought in the shop, your rating and your match history (the moves of your recent games). Only the game, signed in as you, can read these files. We can't download them.

On this computer only: your settings, your room layout, and your Daily Curate bests and streak.

On Steam, public: your Daily Curate scores and your rating on the world leaderboards (see below), and your achievements.

On Steam, as totals: after each game the game adds to a few overall counters (games played, books shelved and the like). We only ever see the sum over all players.

4. Steam

Accounts, friends, invites, lobbies, online play, achievements, stats, leaderboards and Steam Cloud are run by Valve. What Valve does with your data is covered by the Steam privacy policy: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement.

5. Leaderboards and replays are public

When you play the Daily Curate online, your best score that day goes on the world leaderboard with your Steam display name and the shape of your shelf, and the replay of that run (the order of your picks) is attached to it. Anyone who plays the game can see the entry and watch the replay.

Rated games put your rating on the Rating leaderboard, with your number of rated games, your peak and your wins.

Entries stay on the boards while the game does. To have yours removed, write to us with your Steam name and we'll delete it.

6. Playing with others

At a table, the other players see your Steam display name and avatar, your seat colour, your room as you've decorated it, your moves, your rating, and what you write in the table chat.

The table chat isn't saved. It goes from player to player through Steam during the game, and it's gone when the table closes.

Your Steam friends see what you're doing in the game (in the menu, at a table, in a match) through Steam's own status.

7. This website

bookshelves.live doesn't collect anything: no cookies, no analytics, no forms, no tracking. Everything it shows, the trailer included, comes from this site.

It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and its domain and email run on Cloudflare. Like any web host they see your IP address to send you the page, under their own privacy policies. We don't get those logs.

Some links take you to other sites with their own policies: Steam, and our Discord community. Once the game is out, the page shows a small Steam store box loaded from Steam.

8. Email

If you write to us, we get your email address and what you send, in our inbox. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when you ask.

9. Children

The game doesn't ask your age and doesn't collect anything beyond what this page lists. Playing needs a Steam account, which follows Steam's own rules for younger players. A parent can write to us to have a child's leaderboard entries removed.

10. Your choices

See your data: your history, coins and rating are in the game; your leaderboard entries are on the LEADERBOARDS screen.

Delete it: uninstall the game and delete its save folder for what's on this computer; turn off Steam Cloud for Bookshelves, or delete its files in Steam, for what's in the Cloud; write to us for leaderboard entries and replays.

Stay off the leaderboards: play the Daily Curate with Steam offline, or play only with friends or bots. Those games are never posted and don't change your rating.

11. Changes to this page

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be announced in the game's Steam news.
