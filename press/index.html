ABOUT THE GAME

A cozy board game for you and your friends. Draft books from library carts, line them up by genre and fill your shelf for points. Up to 4 players online, with bots for the empty seats, plus a fresh Daily Curate puzzle every day.

Some people collect stamps. Some collect records. You? You collect books.

Bookshelves is a cozy drafting board game for 2 to 4 players. Every book is out in the open. On your turn, take every book of one genre from a library cart, and the rest slide to the rug, up for grabs. Line them up on your dresser, one genre per row. Take more than fits and the extras hit the floor, and that costs points.

Fill a row and one of its books goes up to your bookshelf, scoring for every neighbour it lines up with. Complete rows, columns and whole genres for big bonuses at the end. The first to raid the rug takes the e-reader and goes first next round.

FEATURES

Play with friends, or with anyone

  • Up to 4 players online through Steam. Invite friends or join from the friends list, with no ports to open.
  • Find a Match puts you at a public table. Bots fill any empty seats.
  • A full game or a quick one, and a rating that grows with every rated game.

A new puzzle every day

  • Daily Curate: the same set shelf and the same books for everyone, every day.
  • Keep your streak going, see where you rank on the world leaderboard and watch the top players' replays.

Make the room yours

  • Earn coins as you play and spend them in the shop on cosmetics: plants, fairy lights, a whole aquarium.
  • Lola, the dog, is asleep on the bed. Maybe she has a favourite spot.

Cozy to pick up

  • A guided practice game teaches you the rules in one round.
  • Mouse or controller, your call. Full gamepad support.
  • 21 Steam achievements (a couple of them hidden), in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
